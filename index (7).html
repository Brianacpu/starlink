FAQ


How do I pay?

Enter your phone number at checkout and tap PAY NOW. You will get an M-PESA prompt on your phone. Enter your PIN to approve it.

When will my payment show as successful?

Only after our server receives confirmation from the payment provider. We never mark a payment successful before that.

I did not receive the prompt. What now?

Check that the number is correct and that your phone has network coverage, then try again. If you were charged but see an error, contact us.

Can I use Airtel or Telkom?

Yes. Select your network before buying. Payment is made through M-PESA.
